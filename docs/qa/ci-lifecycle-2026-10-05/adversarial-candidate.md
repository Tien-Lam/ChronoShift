## Actionable finding at this candidate

The new uncontrolled-test console listener stores unexpected serialization errors for `flush()` to surface, but `afterEach` returns early on expected status without calling `flush()`. A test that passes all UI assertions could therefore silently lose a genuine capture error. Move flushing before the successful-status return, retain evidence on capture failure and rethrow the genuine error after attachment. Root was notified independently before its hook-fix delta.

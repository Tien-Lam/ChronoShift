## Remaining caveats and required falsification

1. **Scroll-time pointer blocking remains real.** Installed `Virtualizer.js:54,84` sets allowsWindowScrolling=true internally; public VirtualizerProps expose no disable-scroll or pointer-shield override. `ScrollView.js:80–140` listens to own/ancestor scroll, updates the visible rectangle and starts scrolling state. Its300ms timer at113–121 uses a rescheduling threshold; it is not a promise of exact wall delay from every event. At267 it sets inner content pointerEvents:none while scrolling. Therefore wheel/scroll→immediate option click can be intercepted even in a correctly owned menu. Use normal interactions/actionability and verify the intended exact option commit after realistic scroll, repeated scrolling and keyboard-generated scroll. Do not add a300ms sleep, force-click, override pointerEvents, modify library timers or loosen the exact oracle.
2. **Ancestor scrolling and popup dismissal remain intentional.** `useCloseOnScroll` still dismisses an open overlay when an ancestor of the trigger scrolls, excluding input/textarea internal scrolling. Current corrected preparation addresses one known automation focus-scroll ordering. It does not authorize disabling ancestor dismissal, reopening by the toggle in place of the reported input-driven journey, increasing viewport height as final acceptance, or globally moving the pointer to hide lifecycle defects. Verify both corrected reopening and intentional ancestor-scroll dismissal with normal motion and owned active/exiting lists.
3. **Window intersection and mobile geometry.** With allowsWindowScrolling=true, visible rectangle uses viewport offset, list scroll and browser window size. A transient offscreen/zero rectangle during popup placement can change mounted rows. Native iPhone-height full Home/End/offscreen selection failed both old builds; no broad mobile keyboard verdict exists. First render, entry/exit, trigger near viewport edges, short landscape height and mobile visual-viewport/keyboard changes require live verification. Desktop or taller controls do not resolve this gap.
4. **Resize and measurement feedback.** `ScrollView.js:148–186` guards reentrancy and allows at most two scrollbar-adjustment passes; content-size changes queue a microtask, and border-box observation avoids a known loop. Those defenses are capabilities, not proof the recorded warning is gone. `useVirtualizerItem.js:43–58` observes direct children only and explicitly notes deeper structural changes can leave stale heights. Its measurement at73–76 temporarily clears wrapper height and reads scrollWidth/scrollHeight. Narrow→wide→narrow open resize, font/layout changes, wrapped long descriptions and scroll anchoring must yield correct heights/positions without overlapping/clipped rows or page errors. Preserve and investigate ResizeObserver warnings; do not filter them to achieve a pass.
5. **Empty collection and short filtering.** The ListBox empty placeholder has role=option independently of real choices. Verify no-match UI plus zero **data-value selectable options**, custom text/offset retention, invalid source/target correction and recovery. Do not assert that every role-option disappears, nor remove existing empty-state assertions. Test filter from full→few→none→full at nonzero scroll, focused rows removed by filtering, and selected values outside the current filtered view.
6. **Complete logical choices and focus.** ListBox passes focused persisted keys into CollectionRoot (`ListBox.js:284`); useOption supplies aria-posinset/setsize from the logical collection. Those semantics must be verified with owned aria-controls and aria-activedescendant pointing to a mounted correct descendant, and that descendant's rectangle intersecting the actual scroll viewport. Home/End alone proves boundary access, not every interior choice. Derive the full expected IDs/order/labels/descriptions independently from the unchanged baseline collection per engine; older 454 Chromium / 479 WebKit counts are observations, not universal constants. Verify interior/offscreen aliases, complete logical set size/order, filter results, arrow/Tab/Enter and pointer commit identity. Do not slice options, substitute mounted-row count for total choices, or weaken canonical input/other-field expectations.
7. **Motion, CSP and acceptance scope.** Entry/exit and immediate close remain unchanged. Capture screenshots/traces separately from normal-use CSP records, since old reports show Playwright capture injected stylesheet refusals on both builds. Preserve meaningful console/page errors. Current273 configured browser identities,116 units, independent fixtures and subpath/privacy/offline/update/publication gates remain intact; standalone complementary probes can fill gaps without deleting assertions or claiming physical-device/screen-reader acceptance from ARIA metadata.

Interpret the future Linux output carefully. `logicalCpus`, availableParallelism/affinity and cgroup bandwidth are different observations; totalmem may describe host-visible RAM rather than the cgroup limit. A finite cpu.max is bandwidth quota/period, while max only means no limit at that cgroup. cpu.stat throttling at that level does not necessarily reveal inherited ancestor throttling. memory.peak includes descendants and history since creation unless reset through that descriptor; this script performs no reset. Before/after CPU/event deltas include all activity in the observed cgroup interval, not just browsers or individual cases. Null/missing values are unknown, not zero pressure. These qualifications follow the [kernel's cgroup v2 documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.html); no exact CPU/memory cause is claimed.
